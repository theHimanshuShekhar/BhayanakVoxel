# 5 cm Voxels

The Voxel is 5 cm, not 10 cm. This supersedes the Voxel size in [Choose Voxel size & Planet radius](https://github.com/theHimanshuShekhar/bhayanakvoxel/issues/8); the rest of that decision (the 16 km radius, the depth, gravity, the Zoom range) stands. It was decided with the art direction ([#135](https://github.com/theHimanshuShekhar/bhayanakvoxel/issues/135), item 2) and built in [#147](https://github.com/theHimanshuShekhar/bhayanakvoxel/issues/147). #8 chose 10 cm for headroom on the 4090 and said that 5 cm would be tried later; the art direction wants vegetation and rock detail closer to its reference (Teardown is the evidence for how small Voxels read), and that is worth a Voxel an eighth of the volume.

The size is written down once, as `brick_store::VOXELS_PER_METRE` (20), the lowest crate everything depends on. A test reads every source file and fails on a second definition. Everything else is derived from it:

- **Sizes are in metres, counts are counts.** The generator is written in metres (the 16 km radius, the 2 km peak, the strata, the caves, the sea, the test Planet's relief), so the same seed makes the same landscape at twice the linear resolution: a test samples the ground at fixed latitudes and longitudes and holds it to one 10 cm Voxel of what 10 cm Voxels made. The character (capsule, 0.5 m auto-step, eye height, speeds), the Edit reach (8 m) and the flight speeds keep their metric size (the Brush limit is the exception, below). The Voxel-count budgets keep their counts and so cover an eighth of the volume (below).
- **The world map is the same.** Its elevations are stored in 10 cm units, in an `i16`, which holds 3.27 km; a 5 cm unit would not hold the 2 km peak. They are converted to Voxels where the ground is read. Its noise lattice is also 10 cm, so the map's bytes, and the golden hash of it, did not change. `GENERATOR_VERSION` is 4: everything the map is turned into did.
- **LOD.** `Bands::d0` is in Voxels (the distance at which a Voxel covers one pixel), so it is 1028 Voxels as before and halves in metres: 51 m. Level `L` at 5 cm is the size of level `L - 1` at 10 cm. The pyramid has 14 levels (`render::lod::LEVELS`), 0 to 13, so that level 13's 410 m Voxels are still under a pixel at the 800 km Zoom limit. The Guard is levels 9 to 13: the same Voxel sizes, 25.6 m to 410 m, and so the same bricks, as 8 to 12 were. Caves reach level 7 (Voxels of 6.4 m) and none is in level 8 and up, as in 7 and up.

## Considered Options

- **Stay at 10 cm:** the status quo; the art direction's detail goal is not reachable.
- **A smaller Voxel only near the camera:** two representations of the Planet; rejected by ADR 0002, which has one.
- **Keep the sizes in Voxels and let the world get smaller:** the Planet would be 8 km, and the walk, the reach and the Brushes 2 times smaller than decided.

## Consequences

- **Full-resolution data near a player is about 8 times as much** for the same metres, and the resident set a camera needs is not: the band rule is in Voxels, so the bricks the bands select are about as many, the shell reaches the same metres (the horizon, 253 m) in twice as many Voxels, and `d0` halves. What grows is the cost of the near ground: the 40 m spawn ring is 5.6 million bricks in 7.6 s on eight cores (751 000 in 1.8 s at 10 cm), and a full fill with the camera still generates 23.9 million bricks in 158 s and draws 3.56 million (3.37 d0², 3.04 at 10 cm).
- **The character's tick costs about 5 times as much**: the capsule touches about 7 times the Voxels, and a kilometre's run takes 10.6 to 12.8 ms a tick in the mean on this box (2.1 ms at 10 cm; 10.6 ms with the ground ball held at 4 m, 12.8 ms with it at 8 m), against the 16.7 ms there is. The 99th percentile is 24 ms and the worst tick 33 ms, so the test's 99th-percentile limit fails too, not only its mean. Decided (2026-11-07): #154 brings it back, straight after this ticket. The test `a_character_runs_a_kilometre_from_the_spawn_without_stopping_while_the_ground_keeps_up` keeps its half-tick limit and is `#[ignore]`d until then.
- **The ground stays metric** (decided 2026-11-07): `sim::Ground::REACH` is 8 m (160 Voxels), with a ring buffer of 64 bricks a side and 1024 new bricks a tick at the most (they were 80, 32 and 128). A count held at 80 Voxels (4 m) left the character waiting for its ground and the client corrected on a late leash. Its memory is 8 times as much: `Ground::MAX_BYTES`, the bound, is 308 MB (294 MiB; 38 MB at 10 cm), and the most a ground held in the kilometre walk, on the client and on the server alike, was 14.6 MB (2.4 MB at 10 cm). The server keeps one per player.
- **The Edit rate is the one budget that went metric** (decided in #137, reconfirmed 2026-11-08): 8 m³ a second, `VOXELS_PER_SECOND` 64 000 at 5 cm, derived from `VOXELS_PER_METRE` so that it cannot drift. The Voxel burst (9 seconds' worth, 576 000) and the work rate and burst (4.8 million units a second and 2.88 million, 8 times the 10 cm figures) scale with it and keep their ratios, so a full 2 m-radius dig takes about 4 s and not 33 s. The Brush limits are metric again, 2 m in radius (40 Voxels) and 1 m for a Smooth or a Flatten (20), and are checked against the bursts: `affordable_radius` works out the biggest radius they afford (40, and 22 for a Smooth, which the 1 m limit is below) and a test holds every allowed Edit to them. The cost is on the server: a biggest Brush asks for about 2.1 to 2.9 million units, 21 to 29 ms at 10 ns a unit, in one tick, and `EDIT_WORK_PER_TICK` (twice the burst) is 5.76 million, which is 58 ms, three and a half ticks of server time, if enough players dig at once; the server's tick budget for Edits needs a decision of its own. Whether the 1 ms Edit+BVH stage holds at this rate is for the 4090 bench (see #147: the bench does not exercise Edits yet).
- **Budgets that keep their count change their metric meaning:**
  - the Debris flood fill (1M Voxels) and the Fluids budget (2M active Voxels) cover an eighth of the volume until M3 and M4 tune them;
  - the Voxel counts of the Edit limits no longer keep theirs: see above.
- **Measured constants that were taken at 10 cm are stale and must be measured again:** the client's full-fill figures (`BRICKS_PER_D0_SQUARED`, `REFS_PER_BRICK`, `WORDS_PER_BRICK`, `room_for`'s per-level counts and the tree and waiting counts), the Guard's bytes and the intake and generation rates in `prefetch-sim`'s report. The simulator's calibration of `crust` follows the first of them.
- The coordinates stay far inside `i32` (the Planet's extreme Voxel is 360 000 from the centre; the Zoom at 800 km is 16 million, below the 2^24 that the GPU holds exactly).
- The bands are in Voxels, so the strict band rule (`bias` 1) draws about as many bricks as before (3.37 d0² against 3.04), and the GPU's work follows that and the pixels, not the size of a Voxel. The `client bench` flight is over the test Planet, a fixed number of Voxels, so it measures the same scene as before: the near ground is only in the full-size test.
- `materials.toml`'s `noise_scale` is still in Voxels, as its comment says: the patches that a Material's colour varies over are a Voxel-sized look, so at 5 cm they are half the size in metres. It was left alone on purpose, to be tuned with the look-dev prototype (#148) and not by this switch.
