# 5 cm Voxels

The Voxel is 5 cm, not 10 cm. This supersedes the Voxel size in [Choose Voxel size & Planet radius](https://github.com/theHimanshuShekhar/bhayanakvoxel/issues/8); the rest of that decision (the depth, gravity) stands. The 16 km radius and the Zoom range stood until ADR 0008 (2026-11-10), which makes the Planet 320 km and the Zoom about 20 R. It was decided with the art direction ([#135](https://github.com/theHimanshuShekhar/bhayanakvoxel/issues/135), item 2) and built in [#147](https://github.com/theHimanshuShekhar/bhayanakvoxel/issues/147). #8 chose 10 cm for headroom on the 4090 and said that 5 cm would be tried later; the art direction wants vegetation and rock detail closer to its reference (Teardown is the evidence for how small Voxels read), and that is worth a Voxel an eighth of the volume.

The size is written down once, as `brick_store::VOXELS_PER_METRE` (20), the lowest crate everything depends on. A test reads every source file and fails on a second definition. Everything else is derived from it:

- **Sizes are in metres, counts are counts.** The generator is written in metres (the radius, the 2 km peak, the strata, the caves, the sea, the test Planet's relief), so the same seed makes the same landscape at twice the linear resolution: a test samples the ground at fixed latitudes and longitudes and holds it to one 10 cm Voxel of what 10 cm Voxels made. The character (capsule, 0.5 m auto-step, eye height, speeds), the Edit reach (8 m) and the flight speeds keep their metric size (the Brush limit is the exception, below). The Voxel-count budgets keep their counts and so cover an eighth of the volume (below).
- **The world map is the same.** Its elevations are stored in 10 cm units, in an `i16`, which holds 3.27 km; a 5 cm unit would not hold the 2 km peak. They are converted to Voxels where the ground is read. Its noise lattice is also 10 cm, so the map's bytes, and the golden hash of it, did not change. `GENERATOR_VERSION` is 4: everything the map is turned into did.
- **LOD.** `Bands::d0` is in Voxels (the distance at which a Voxel covers one pixel), so it is 1028 Voxels as before and halves in metres: 51 m. Level `L` at 5 cm is the size of level `L - 1` at 10 cm. The pyramid has 14 levels (`render::lod::LEVELS`), 0 to 13, so that level 13's 410 m Voxels are still under a pixel at the 800 km Zoom limit. The Guard is levels 9 to 13: the same Voxel sizes, 25.6 m to 410 m, and so the same bricks, as 8 to 12 were. Caves reach level 7 (Voxels of 6.4 m) and none is in level 8 and up, as in 7 and up.

The measured costs behind this ADR are in [`docs/measurements.md`](../measurements.md#5-cm-voxels-adr-0007).

## Considered Options

- **Stay at 10 cm:** the status quo; the art direction's detail goal is not reachable.
- **A smaller Voxel only near the camera:** two representations of the Planet; rejected by ADR 0002, which has one.
- **Keep the sizes in Voxels and let the world get smaller:** the Planet would be 8 km, and the walk, the reach and the Brushes 2 times smaller than decided.

## Consequences

- **Full-resolution data near a player is about 8 times as much** for the same metres, and the resident set a camera needs is not: the band rule is in Voxels, so the bricks the bands select are about as many, the shell reaches the same metres in twice as many Voxels, and `d0` halves. What grows is the cost of the near ground (the spawn ring and the fill). #142 stopped generating the children that can never be drawn (air and buried ones), which cut the fill's generation to well under half.
- **The character's tick costs about 5 times as much**, since the capsule touches about 7 times the Voxels, and it did not fit the tick. Decided (2026-11-07): #154 brings it back, straight after this ticket.
- **The ground stays metric** (decided 2026-11-07): `sim::Ground::REACH` is 8 m. A count held at 80 Voxels (4 m) left the character waiting for its ground and the client corrected on a late leash. Its memory bound is 8 times as much, and the server keeps one per player.
- **The Edit rate is the one budget that went metric** (decided in #137, reconfirmed 2026-11-08): 8 m³ a second, derived from `VOXELS_PER_METRE` so that it cannot drift. The Voxel burst and the work rate and burst scale with it and keep their ratios. The Brush limits are metric again, 2 m in radius and 1 m for a Smooth or a Flatten, and are checked against the bursts: `affordable_radius` works out the biggest radius they afford and a test holds every allowed Edit to them. Since #156 the bench exercises Edits, and the chain was cut down to fit the frame. The server's tick budget for Edits when many players dig at once is still undecided; the bot soak measures it first.
- **Budgets that keep their count change their metric meaning:** the Debris flood fill (1M Voxels) and the Fluids budget (2M active Voxels) cover an eighth of the volume until M3 and M4 tune them.
- **Deep water is solid to the generator's proofs, and M1b must revisit that** (decided 2026-11-09, #142). A brick wholly under the sea is proven solid below (`Planet::solid_below`: water and ice are solid Voxels), so it is sealed when its neighbours are, and is neither generated nor drawn. It was the larger part of what #142 saved. That is right while water is opaque. When water becomes transparent (M1b, `transparency` in `materials.toml`) the deep water is seen through the surface, so sealing it hides what a ray should reach: that milestone must stop proving the deep sea solid for the renderer, or generate and draw it to the depth that light reaches, and measure the cost again.
- **Measured constants that were taken at 10 cm are stale and must be measured again:** the client's full-fill figures (`BRICKS_PER_D0_SQUARED`, `REFS_PER_BRICK`, `WORDS_PER_BRICK`, `room_for`'s per-level counts and the tree and waiting counts), the Guard's bytes and the intake and generation rates in `prefetch-sim`'s report. The simulator's calibration of `crust` follows the first of them.
- The coordinates stay far inside `i32`, and at 16 km the Zoom stays below the 2^24 that the GPU holds exactly. ADR 0008 ends this: at 320 km the scene bound and far ray lengths go to coarse units.
- The bands are in Voxels, so the strict band rule (`bias` 1) draws about as many bricks as before, and the GPU's work follows that and the pixels, not the size of a Voxel. The `client bench` flight is over the test Planet, a fixed number of Voxels, so it measures the same scene as before: the near ground is only in the full-size test.
- `materials.toml`'s `noise_scale` is still in Voxels, as its comment says: the patches that a Material's colour varies over are a Voxel-sized look, so at 5 cm they are half the size in metres. It was left alone on purpose, to be tuned with the look-dev prototype (#148) and not by this switch.
